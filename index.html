How the space is made

Four synthesized layers travel at different speeds around a virtual listener. HRTF panning adds directional cues; a generated stereo room softens the edges. Slowly detuned open fifths, vowel-like formant tones, irregular chime phrases and filtered brown noise build the texture.

Everything is generated on your device. Movement runs in the audio graph, so it continues when the visual animation is paused in a background tab.

The reference and the limits

Inspired by Endel’s 8D Odyssey. Its public web player identifies the sound bank as 8d_voice and requests an HLS stream based on the selected mode and hour. The exact audio-generation engine is server side. The playlist was accessible; its AAC segments returned HTTP 403 during this study.

This is an original interpretation of spatial-audio techniques, with original notes and synthesis. It is not a recovered Endel composition or an exact sonic match. No Endel recordings or artwork are included.
